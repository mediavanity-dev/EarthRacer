Drive anywhere on Earth. Every street, building, bridge and hill in EarthRacer comes from real open map data, built live around you as you go, with the real weather, the real time of day and the real speed limits.

ONE RULE: CARRY ON FROM WHERE YOU STOPPED
Pick a starting point once. After that, the world remembers exactly where you parked. Want to see somewhere new? Drive there, or take a real bus, train, ferry or plane from a real stop, in real time.

THINGS TO DO
• Deliveries between real shops and cafés, against the clock.
• A daily race through real streets, against your own ghost.
• Portals: once an hour one opens somewhere in your town for ten minutes. Follow the star on the map and drive through for big points and a one-way trip to the city on its label.
• Random placement: get dropped somewhere on Earth with no labels, read the signs, and guess where you are.
• Collect postcards of landmarks, fill a street atlas, cross bridges, earn feats.

VEHICLES WITH REAL TRADE-OFFS
Bicycle, car, pickup, motorcycle, delivery truck, speedboat, jet ski and a paramotor, each with its own speed, handling, toughness and fuel range. Earn the odd ones too: a 200 mph Speed Demon, a hang glider that rides the wind off hilltops, a rubber duck that swims, waddles and flies, and a dog that swims across rivers. Honk, ring, bark or quack with the horn button.

EXPLORER OR GAMER
• Explorer: the real world, with a few ramps and stunts to find.
• Gamer: ramps, coins, boost arrows, cones, signs to knock down and trees to topple.

REAL CONDITIONS
Rain and snow cut grip, fog closes in, headwinds slow you down, night falls with the real sun. Traffic drives on the correct side for the country. Run out of fuel and you'll need a tow truck, another vehicle, or the emergency solar buggy.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Tilt the phone like a steering wheel, or use the slider or buttons; a keyboard or game controller works too. Swing the camera round with a drag and zoom with a pinch. Tune in to local radio stations wherever you are.

FREE
EarthRacer is free, with no ads and nothing to buy: every vehicle and feat is earned by playing. Long play earns coin rewards and paint jobs. Optional tips in Settings help keep it going; they don't change the game.

OPEN DATA
Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, served by OpenFreeMap. Terrain from AWS Terrain Tiles, weather from Open-Meteo, place names from Nominatim, postcards from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, radio stations from Radio Browser.

PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your game stays on your phone.
