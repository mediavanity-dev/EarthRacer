New vehicles
• Speed Demon: 200 mph, no speed limits, and it stays planted on the road.
• Hang glider: launch from a hilltop and ride the wind up a ridge.
• A rubber duck that's faster than the boats, waddles ashore and flaps into short flights.
• A dog that paddles across water (and has a habit with trees).

Better driving
• Swing and zoom the camera: drag or pinch, the mouse wheel, Q/E and Z/X, or a controller's right stick.
• Bikes now lean into turns.
• The speedometer shows your real speed over the ground.
• Wheels steer and roll smoothly. The handbrake button now says what it's for: handbrake / drift.
• A short drift is free; hold one for more than 5 seconds and your tyres wear.

Sounds
• Horn button (H key, or X on a controller): a real recorded horn pitched for each vehicle, a bicycle bell, a bark, and a cartoon quack.
• Radio while you guess where you are after a random drop (the station's name stays hidden until you guess).
• Quieter rain and wind.

A truer world
• Real, individually mapped trees, including street trees along the kerb.
• Buildings show what they're used for: shopfronts with sign bands on shops and cafés (even in industrial estates), ribbed metal sheds, office glazing, schools and hospitals, and homes.
• Truer roof shapes, and paths that cross water on decks.
• Roads no longer draw over your vehicle.

Long play
• New feats for many hours, days and streaks, with coin rewards and paint jobs for your vehicles.
