New
• Skydiving: bail out of a flight and steer your freefall over the real ground, then float down under a canopy and drive on from where you land.
• Cruises: sail between cruise ports in real time, and watch the coast from the top deck.
• Autopilot, earned with 200 km of motorway driving: it holds your lane and the speed limit on motorways and major highways.
• An odometer for each vehicle, at the bottom of the minimap.
• Portals now lead to about 250 places, and won't send you back somewhere you've just been.
• Nine new seatback games, each for its kind of trip. Train: Spot it, Timetable. Plane: Country quiz, Which way? Bus: Next stop. Cruise: Shuffleboard, Bingo night, Port quiz. Bus, train and ferry: Higher or lower.
• One steering sensitivity setting for every way of steering: keys, controller, tilt, slider and buttons.
• Split screen on a computer: one player on the keyboard, the other on a controller.
• Trains run on the railway lines, about as often as real ones: main-line trains, metros, light rail and trams. They don't stop for you, so look both ways at level crossings.
• Street view: tap 📷 to see street photos of where you are (from Mapillary), or open Google Street View there.
• Outrun the map and a giant quarter-pipe turns you round while it catches up.
• Gamer mode: giant sky tracks about every mile. Ride up from the street, through a loop and two banked bends, and land back on the road for coins.
• Railway track you can see: ballast, sleepers and rails. Trains follow the time of day, busiest at rush hour and rare in the small hours.
• Snow on high mountains, and weather corrected for your height, so a summit is cold and snowy.
• Church towers with spires, and domes and onion domes where the map has them.
• Real planes overhead in the app, at their real height and heading, with contrails up high (live open flight data).
• Viewpoints and lighthouses to reach, for coins and a new feat. In Gamer mode, speed cameras: pass under the limit for coins, or get flashed.
• A note when a real earthquake struck nearby today (from USGS).

Better
• Crash into a car and it gets shoved, spins and dents, by weight: a truck sends it flying, a bike barely moves it.
• Driving into water makes a big splash and stops you, instead of bouncing you off like a wall.
• The duck, the dog, the boats and the jet ski leave a wake.
• The back of a stunt ramp is solid now.
• Real horn recordings every time you press the horn, including right after switching apps, and a fuller double honk for cars.
• Flights: one minute to board and one to get off, with a countdown on screen.
• On a phone that gets hot, the game draws less until it cools (Settings: Keep the phone cool).
• Rain falls in streaks and snow in soft flakes.
• Name the right town in a random placement and it ends there with your score; naming the right country or region tells you so and lets you narrow it down.
• The camera no longer pumps in and out passing buildings.
• Vehicles tilt with the slope, so wheels stay out of hillsides and road edges.
• Street view looks up to 300 m away for photos, and says plainly when there are none.
• Radio stations that play adverts for your real location are listed last.
• A new portal tells you how far away it is.

Fixed
• Swerving could push vehicles past their top speed (the Speed Demon past 400 mph). Top speeds are now firm.
• No-damage mode no longer dents your vehicle.
• Getting off a bus or train early works every time.
• A drift starts even if you press the handbrake before steering (some keyboards drop a third key held at once).
• Buildings and shop signs no longer flicker where two were drawn in the same place.
• Cooler and kinder to the battery: the game no longer draws faster than it needs to.
• At very high speed the game holds less of the map in memory, so phones are less likely to close it.
• On a keyboard, if a drift won't start, the game suggests Shift for the handbrake (many keyboards can't read Space, W and an arrow at once).
